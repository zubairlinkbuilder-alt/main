# Project notes for Claude

## Magazine ideas / magazine content — required format

When the user asks for magazine ideas or magazine content for their websites
(ExerciseMenu.com or others), use the **long-form editorial feature** format of
WellnessVoice Magazine, not short checklists or tip lists.

Reference pages the user shared: a "Conversation" feature ("Nathalie Botros:
The Power of Choosing Happiness") with a story intro, Q&A, pull quotes and
full-page photos.

### Content structure per feature
1. **Section label** at the top — e.g. `CONVERSATION`, `FEATURE`, `COACH'S CORNER`.
2. **Headline in two parts** — the person's name + a big thematic line
   (e.g. "SADIA BALOCH: / WHY BEGINNERS QUIT IN WEEK THREE").
3. **Byline** — "By [writer], [role]" with a small round headshot.
4. **Story intro (2–4 paragraphs)** opening with a drop cap: who the person is,
   what they're known for, credentials and recognition, their mission in one line.
5. **Q&A body** — questions in bold coloured caps (`Q: …`), answers starting with
   the person's name in bold. 5–8 questions that move from origin story →
   turning point → method/habits → advice for the reader → what's next.
6. **Answers carry real detail**: personal story, specific routines, concrete
   examples, numbers where honest. The reader should leave with something they
   can apply, not only inspiration.
7. **Pull quotes** — 1–2 per spread, large serif, pulled from the answers.
8. **Closing line** — a forward-looking sentence from the person.

### Layout / design
- Two-page spreads: text in 3 narrow justified columns on one side, a full-bleed
  photo (or a photo with a large pull quote over it) on the other.
- Serif body text, bold sans headlines, one accent colour for questions and the drop cap.
- Footer on every page: page number + "[Magazine name] | [Edition]".
- Flipbook viewer: page counter ("8-9 / 10"), left/right arrows, PDF download.

### Honesty rules
- Never invent quotes, awards, press mentions, client numbers or life stories for
  a real person. Anything that isn't in material the user gave you goes in as a clearly
  marked placeholder (e.g. `[SADIA: answer about first client]`) or as a question
  list for the interview.
- Photos: use the user's real photos; otherwise leave marked photo slots, never
  stock images presented as the person.
