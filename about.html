About Us

Our Story & Legacy

Over five decades of manufacturing excellence in Pakistan's paper & packaging industry.

Since 1972

Manufacturing Excellence for Over 50 Years

Surayya Aslam Paper is a licensed manufacturer (License No. 1066150) active in Pakistan's paper industry. Based in Multan's Industrial Estate, our company operates with an international scope, producing and exporting high-quality packaging materials worldwide.

With Trader Identification Number Pk41938429 assigned by the World Customs Organization (WCO), we adhere to international standards for identifying foreign operators — exporters, suppliers, manufacturers — ensuring efficient cross-border trade facilitation and supply chain control.

Our core business activities include:

  • Production and sale of all kinds of packaging paper
  • Production and sale of cartons and cardboard
  • Production and printing of packaging boxes
  • Importing waste paper for sustainable production
Our Journey

Key Milestones

1972 — Foundation

Surayya Aslam Paper established in Multan, Pakistan, beginning operations in the packaging paper industry.

1980s — Expansion

Production capacity expanded with additional machinery. First export orders fulfilled, marking the beginning of international trade.

1990s — Corrugated Division

Cardboard-making and converting units established, adding corrugated cartons and cardboard to our product range.

2000s — Modern Infrastructure

Major facility upgrades with PM3 and PM4 production lines. Daily capacity reached 250 tons per day.

2010s — Global Trade

WCO certification obtained (TIN: Pk41938429). Active participation in international trade exhibitions across multiple continents.

Today — Industry Leader

Operating 4 production lines producing all paper grades from 100–600 GSM, with 5-color printing capability for custom packaging.
