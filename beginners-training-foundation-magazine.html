The Basics

Reps & Sets — The Foundation of Every Workout

If you don't understand this, nothing else will make sense.

REP
Repetition

A REP (repetition) is one complete movement — one squat, one push-up, one curl.

SET
A group of reps

A SET is a group of reps performed back to back before resting.

3×10
Rest
Rest

So 3x10 means 3 sets of 10 reps.

Why it matters: the number of reps you do directly determines what your body adapts to. Change the reps, change the result.

Remember3 sets of 10 reps = 3x10. Simple. But the number you pick changes everything.

03 · StructureSession Blueprint
Session Blueprint

What a Proper Workout Actually Looks Like

Most people skip the parts that matter most.

  1. 1

    Warm-Up 5–10 MIN

    Light cardio + dynamic stretches. Never skip this.

  2. 2

    Compound Movements First

    Squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows. Big muscles, big energy.

  3. 3

    Isolation Exercises After

    Curls, lateral raises, tricep pushdowns.

  4. 4

    Cool-Down 5 MIN

    Static stretching. Speeds up recovery.

RuleHardest exercise goes first. You have the most energy at the start — use it on what matters.

04 · ProgressionProgressive Overload
Progressive Overload

Why You Need to Keep Challenging Your Body

Same weight every week = zero progress.

Progressive overload means gradually increasing the demand on your muscles over time.

Without it, your body has no reason to change.

Week 1Slightly more each week

How to apply it as a beginner

  • Add 1–2 reps before adding weight
  • When you hit the top of your rep range for all sets, add 2.5–5kg
  • Track every session — if you're not recording it, you're guessing
The weekly goalThe goal every week: do slightly more than last week. That's it.

06 · Avoid TheseCommon Beginner Mistakes
Common Beginner Mistakes

5 Mistakes That Kill Beginner Progress

You're probably making at least two of these.

  1. 01

    Too Much Too Soon

    6 days a week in month one = burnout or injury. Start with 3.

  2. 02

    Junk Volume

    More sets ≠ more results. 10 sets of curls doesn't build more muscle than 4 quality sets.

  3. 03

    No Progression Plan

    Showing up without tracking means you'll do the same thing forever.

  4. 04

    Skipping Warm-Ups

    Cold muscles + heavy weight = injury waiting to happen.

  5. 05

    Copying Advanced Programs

    If it's designed for someone with 3 years of training, it's not for you yet.
