Who We Are

Pakistan's Trusted Paper Manufacturer

Surayya Aslam Group is a diversified conglomerate established in 1972, active in Pakistan's paper, packaging, and industrial sectors. With international trade operations and WCO certification (TIN: Pk41938429), we supply packaging solutions across borders.

Our Multan facility houses four production lines (PM1–PM4) producing Kraft Liner, Test Liner, Fluting papers, corrugated cartons, and cardboard in grammages from 100 to 600 GSM.
