Our Story

Manufacturing Excellence Since 1972

Surayya Aslam Paper is a manufacturer active in the paper industry with license number 1066150 and Trader Identification Number (TIN) Pk41938429. Based in Multan, Pakistan, our business activities span internationally.

With 4 production lines (PM1 to PM4) and a daily capacity of 250 tons, we produce Kraft Liner, Test Liner Super Type, Kraft Liner B-Type, Fluting papers, corrugated cartons, and cardboard in all standard widths and GSM ranges from 100 to 600 grams.
