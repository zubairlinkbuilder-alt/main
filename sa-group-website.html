Who We Are

Pakistan's Trusted Paper & Packaging Conglomerate

Surayya Aslam Group is a licensed manufacturer (License No. 1066150) and one of Pakistan's leading paper and packaging conglomerates. Based in Multan's Industrial Estate, our group operates with an international scope, producing and exporting high-quality packaging materials worldwide.

With Trader Identification Number Pk41938429 assigned by the World Customs Organization (WCO), we adhere to international standards for cross-border trade facilitation and supply chain control.

Our core business activities include:

  • Production and sale of all kinds of packaging paper
  • Production and sale of cartons and cardboard
  • Production and printing of packaging boxes
  • Importing waste paper for sustainable production
  • Industrial manufacturing, cones, and agro products

Our Journey

Key Milestones

1972 — Foundation

Surayya Aslam Paper established in Multan, Pakistan, beginning operations in the packaging paper industry.

1980s — Expansion

Production capacity expanded with additional machinery. First export orders fulfilled, marking the beginning of international trade.

1990s — Corrugated Division

Cardboard-making and converting units established, adding corrugated cartons and cardboard to our product range.

2000s — Modern Infrastructure

Major facility upgrades with PM3 and PM4 production lines. Daily capacity reached 250 tons per day.

2010s — Global Trade & Diversification

WCO certification obtained. Group expanded into industrial manufacturing, agro products, and automation technologies.

Today — 14+ Companies

SA Group now comprises 14+ companies spanning paper mills, packaging, industrial manufacturing, agro products, and automation.
