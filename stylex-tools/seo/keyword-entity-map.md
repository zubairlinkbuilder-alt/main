## Entity map

**Face shape taxonomy:** oval, round, square, oblong/rectangle, heart,
diamond, triangle/pear

**Facial anatomy:** forehead, hairline, temple, cheekbone, zygomatic arch,
jawline, gonial angle, chin, facial thirds, facial width-to-height ratio

**Measurement:** facial landmark, 68-point landmark model, iBUG 300-W,
landmark detection, face detection, ratio, aspect ratio, symmetry, head pose,
roll/yaw/pitch

**Haircuts (men):** taper fade, mid fade, high fade, low taper, buzz cut, crew
cut, French crop, textured crop, quiff, pompadour, faux hawk, angular fringe,
side part, undercut

**Haircuts (women):** bob, lob, blunt bob, pixie cut, shag, long layers,
curtain bangs, side-swept bangs, blunt fringe, face-framing layers

**Facial hair:** stubble, full beard, goatee, beard line, cheek line, beard
fade

**Age estimation:** apparent age, chronological age, biological age,
perceived age, age estimation, age assurance, age verification, MAE (mean
absolute error), CNN, vision transformer, deep learning

**Organisations / products in the space:** FaceAge (Mass General Brigham /
Harvard AIM, published in *The Lancet Digital Health*), Rigr AI, Australian
Government Age Assurance Technology Trial, Yoti, face-api.js, MediaPipe,
TensorFlow.js

**Regulatory:** Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, GDPR Article 9, special-category
data, biometric identifier, informed consent

## Internal linking

The tool pages are hubs, not dead ends.

- Every shape-specific haircut article links **into** the detector with the
  anchor "find your face shape".
- The detector's result card links **out** to the article for the detected
  shape ,  add those URLs to `LINKS` once they are live in the sitemap.
- The age tool links to the face shape detector. That handoff is the whole
  reason the age tool is worth hosting: it converts off-topic curiosity
  traffic into traffic on a cluster you actually monetise.
