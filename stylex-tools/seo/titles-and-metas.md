# Titles & meta descriptions

Lengths are measured, not estimated. Working limits: title ≤ 60 characters,
meta description 140–160. These go in your SEO plugin (Rank Math / Yoast) on
the WordPress page ,  not into the tool's HTML file, which your theme and
plugin override anyway.

title sharpens relevance for male queries but drops you out of the female

53-character alternate and build a separate female page later ,  that is the
one case where a second page is not cannibalization, because the intent
differs.

## Why one page, not two

`face shape detector` and `face shape calculator` are the same task under two
names, so they belong on one URL.

| Term | Suggestion variants | What dominates |
|---|---|---|
| face shape detector | 399 | Brand modifiers: hiface, lenskart, facetune, yesglasses, airbrush |
| face shape calculator | 142 | Method intent: omni, by measurements, measure, face shape formula |

Both sets contain `from photo`, `ai`, `free` and `app` variants. Same intent,
different vocabulary. Cover both on `/face-shape-calculator/` with an H2 and
an FAQ entry using the "detector" phrasing, rather than a second page.
